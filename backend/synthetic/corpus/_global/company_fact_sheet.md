# About Rockridge Power & Light Company

*Appendix A — Company Overview*

---

## Overview

Rockridge Power & Light Company (RPL) is an Indiana investor-owned electric distribution utility incorporated under the laws of the State of Indiana and regulated by the Indiana Utility Regulatory Commission (IURC) as a public utility under IC 8-1-2. RPL is a wholly owned subsidiary of Rockridge Energy Group, Inc. The Company's principal offices are located at 400 Wabash Commons Drive, Lafayette, Indiana 47901.

RPL is exclusively an electric distribution company. It owns no generating units. The Company purchases full-requirements wholesale power under a FERC-jurisdictional supply agreement and operates within the MISO footprint. RPL's owned infrastructure includes 69 kV subtransmission facilities (418 circuit miles) and 12.47 kV and 34.5 kV primary distribution facilities.

---

## Service Territory

RPL provides retail electric distribution service to approximately 407,491 customers across 14 counties in the west-central region of Indiana. The service territory encompasses the following counties: Tippecanoe, Clinton, Carroll, White, Benton, Warren, Fountain, Montgomery, Boone, Hendricks, Putnam, Parke, Vermillion, and Vigo.

The Company maintains five service centers to support field operations, meter services, and customer contact across the territory:

## Organization Summary

RPL employs approximately 1,450 people. The Company's executive leadership team includes the President & Chief Operating Officer, Vice Presidents for Operations, Regulatory & Government Affairs, and Legal. Key operational departments include Distribution Operations, Metering, Customer Operations, Environmental Health & Safety, Compliance, and Regulatory Affairs.

Field crews for line and service work are internal RPL employees. Vegetation management work along RPL rights-of-way is performed by two contracted line-clearance firms. Emergency environmental response services are provided by a 24/7 contracted environmental response firm.

The Company's fleet consists of approximately 640 vehicles, including 210 aerial/bucket and digger-derrick units. Each of the five service centers maintains vehicle fueling capability.

RPL operates a Meter Shop & Standards Laboratory at the Lafayette HQ campus, where meter accuracy testing and standards maintenance are conducted. The Distribution Control Center (DCC), also located in Lafayette, operates on a 24/7 basis and serves as the nerve center for grid monitoring, switching, and outage response coordination across the entire service territory.

## Key Systems

RPL operates an integrated suite of utility operating systems:

| System | Function |
|---|---|
| Customer Information System (CIS) | Billing, account management, customer records, service orders |
| Outage Management System (OMS) | Outage detection, crew dispatch, restoration tracking, customer notification |
| AMI Head-End System (AMI HES) | Two-way communication with 371,200 advanced meters for usage data, remote connects/disconnects, outage notification (last-gasp) |
| Geographic Information System (GIS) | Network mapping, asset location, environmental screening layers |
| Work & Asset Management (WAM) | Field work orders, asset records, maintenance tracking, PCB status codes |
| EHS Incident Management System (EHS-IMS) | Environmental spill records, safety incident tracking, CAPA management |
| Document Control System (DCS) | Controlled document repository, revision history, distribution |
