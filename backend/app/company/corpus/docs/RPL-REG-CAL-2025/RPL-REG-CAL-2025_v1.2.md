## Section 1 — Purpose

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:1.1 -->
**1.1** This document establishes Rockridge Power & Light Company's (RPL) 2025 Regulatory Compliance Calendar, providing a structured schedule of all regulatory filing deadlines, reporting milestones, compliance review cycles, internal audit checkpoints, and notice obligations arising from RPL's status as an Indiana investor-owned electric distribution utility subject to jurisdiction of the Indiana Utility Regulatory Commission (IURC) under IC 8-1-2, and subject to the rules promulgated thereunder in Title 170 of the Indiana Administrative Code (170 IAC).

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:1.2 -->
**1.2** The calendar serves as the primary annual operating reference for RPL's Regulatory Affairs department and coordinates with the Compliance, Legal, Customer Operations, Metering, Distribution Operations, and Environmental Health & Safety departments to ensure that no regulatory obligation is missed. Each event in the calendar is assigned an owner who is accountable for timely completion and documentation.

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:1.3 -->
**1.3** This version (v1.2) covers calendar year 2025 (January 1 through December 31, 2025) and was prepared using law as-of date 2024-12-31 (Snapshot S1). As noted in the front-matter lagging notice, sections 170 IAC 1-6-3, 1-6-4, and 1-6-5 were amended effective 2025-01-24 and are not reflected in this version. The calendar owner (P09) shall prepare a v1.3 update in Q2 2025 to incorporate those changes.

## Section 2 — Scope

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:2.1 -->
**2.1 Jurisdictional Scope.** This calendar covers all compliance obligations applicable to RPL as an Indiana investor-owned electric distribution utility under IC 8-1-2, IC 8-1-1, and the rules codified in 170 IAC Articles 1 and 4. RPL holds IURC Utility ID 99012 and serves approximately 407,491 customers across 14 west-central Indiana counties. RPL is classified as an electric distribution–only utility; it does not own generating units and operates within the MISO footprint under a FERC-jurisdictional full-requirements wholesale power agreement.

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:2.2 -->
**2.2 Regulatory Instruments Covered.** The calendar tracks obligations arising from the following regulatory authorities as of law-as-of date 2024-12-31: (a) 170 IAC 1-2 (Tariff Schedule Filing Requirements); (b) 170 IAC 1-5 (Expedited Rate Case Procedures); (c) 170 IAC 1-6 (30-Day Administrative Filing Procedures); (d) 170 IAC 4-1 (Standards of Service for Electrical Utilities); and (e) applicable requirements of IC 8-1-2 and IC 8-1-37.

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:2.3 -->
**2.3 Exclusions.** This calendar does not cover: (a) FERC-jurisdictional obligations under RPL's wholesale power agreement; (b) MISO membership and settlement reporting, which are tracked separately by RPL's Power Supply function; (c) SEC or federal securities reporting requirements, which are the responsibility of RPL's parent entity Rockridge Energy Group, Inc.; (d) IDEM environmental permit reporting, which is tracked in RPL's environmental management program; and (e) IOSHA safety reporting, tracked in RPL's EHS Incident Management System.

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:2.4 -->
**2.4 Relationship to Other Documents.** This calendar supplements and cross-references RPL's Wave 2 implementation procedures including RPL-TAR-GRR-012 (General Rate Rules Tariff), RPL-CS-PRO-004 (Customer Service Disconnection and Deposits), RPL-CS-PRO-007 (Metering Procedures), RPL-CS-PRO-011 (Complaint Handling), RPL-MTR-PGM-001 (Meter Testing Program), RPL-CMP-REG-001 (Regulatory Compliance Program), RPL-LEG-RRS-001 (Records Retention Schedule), RPL-DO-PLN-002 (Distribution Operations Plan), and RPL-DCC-PRO-003 (Distribution Control Center Procedures).

## Section 3 — Definitions

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:3.1 -->
**3.1 "Filing deadline"** means the date by which RPL must transmit a document, tariff sheet, petition, or report to the IURC such that it is received and file-stamped by the Secretary of the Commission on or before that date, consistent with 170 IAC 1-6-2(8) and IURC electronic portal requirements.

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:3.2 -->
**3.2 "Reporting cycle"** means a recurring schedule (monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, or annual) pursuant to which RPL must generate and submit or retain a compliance report, data summary, or self-assessment, as prescribed by applicable 170 IAC rules or RPL internal policy.

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:3.3 -->
**3.3 "Compliance window"** means the period within which RPL must take a required action, measured from a triggering event. For example, under 170 IAC 4-1-11(d), RPL must deliver a written meter test report to a requesting customer within ten (10) days after test completion.

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:3.4 -->
**3.4 "Notice period"** means the advance time required before an action takes effect or before a filing is considered approved. Under 170 IAC 1-6-1(d), the minimum notice period for approval of administrative rate changes is thirty (30) calendar days from the date the filing is received by the Commission.

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:3.5 -->
**3.5 "IURC business day"** means any weekday (Monday through Friday) that is not a State of Indiana holiday, as observed by IURC operations at the PNC Center, 101 W. Washington Street, Suite 1500E, Indianapolis, IN 46204. IURC business hours are 8:15 a.m. to 4:45 p.m. ET.

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:3.6 -->
**3.6 "RPL company holiday"** means a day on which RPL Lafayette HQ and field operations are not open for normal business. 2025 RPL company holidays are: January 1, January 20, May 26, July 4, September 1, November 27, November 28, December 24, and December 25.

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:3.7 -->
**3.7 "Administrative filing"** means a filing made under 170 IAC 1-6 for noncontroversial changes to rates, charges, rules, or regulations that do not require a full evidentiary hearing. Per 170 IAC 1-6-2(10), a noncontroversial filing is any filing regarding which no person or entity has filed an objection under section 7 of that rule.

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:3.8 -->
**3.8 "General rate case"** means a proceeding under 170 IAC 1-5 initiated by RPL's election to file a petition for a general rate change, supported by a case-in-chief and working papers. The expected time frame for completion is ten (10) months per 170 IAC 1-5-2.1(a).

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:3.9 -->
**3.9 "Tariff schedule"** means a schedule of rates, tolls, charges, rules, and regulations on file with the IURC, filed under RPL's IURC series designation for electric service (IURC No. E). Each schedule must be in 8.5 x 11 inch format per 170 IAC 1-2-3. Separate schedules must be filed for each service and location per 170 IAC 1-2-1.

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:3.10 -->
**3.10 "Internal compliance review"** means an RPL-initiated assessment of compliance status against applicable regulatory requirements, conducted by the Regulatory Affairs or Compliance department, resulting in a documented finding. Internal reviews are RPL practice unless otherwise specified by an IURC order or IAC rule.

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:3.11 -->
**3.11 "Meter testing cycle"** means the periodic schedule under which RPL tests in-service watthour meters, per the election made under 170 IAC 4-1-10. RPL has adopted Method B (quality control testing), and annually draws a sample from each lot per ANSI/ASQC Standard Z1.9.

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:3.12 -->
**3.12 "Lagging_at_approval"** means a document-level flag indicating that one or more regulatory provisions cited in the document were amended after the law-as-of date used for drafting but before the document's approval date, such that the document does not reflect the most current law as of its approval date. This flag is set in the document's basis.json and front matter.

## Section 4 — Using This Calendar

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:4.1 -->
**4.1 Column Structure.** Each calendar event in Sections 5–9 is presented in a table with the following columns: (a) **Event ID** — unique identifier in format EVT-2025-nnnn; (b) **Event Title** — short descriptive name; (c) **Citation** — applicable 170 IAC or IC citation from the T11 grounding pack; (d) **Type** — one of: filing, reporting, internal_review, notice, or inspection; (e) **Due Date** — specific 2025 date or "ongoing" for continuous obligations; (f) **Owner** — person ID and name from the RPL people directory; and (g) **Notes** — date-calculation basis, business-day adjustments, or lagging flags.

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:4.2 -->
**4.2 Owner Responsibilities.** The event owner listed for each calendar event is responsible for: (a) initiating the preparation process at least 30 days before the due date; (b) coordinating with subject-matter experts to compile required data; (c) obtaining required internal approvals before external filing; (d) submitting the filing or report to the IURC or other regulatory body; (e) retaining a copy of all filings and confirmations per RPL-LEG-RRS-001; and (f) updating this calendar's event status in the tracking system within 2 business days of completion.

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:4.3 -->
**4.3 Business Day Adjustment.** When a due date falls on an Indiana state holiday, an IURC non-business day, or an RPL company holiday, the due date is advanced to the next IURC business day. This rule applies to all external filings. For internal review milestones, the due date is advanced to the next RPL business day.

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:4.4 -->
**4.4 Update Process.** This calendar shall be reviewed and updated at the beginning of each calendar quarter (target dates: January 15, April 15, July 15, October 15). The calendar owner (P09) shall: (a) verify that all Q-year events remain current; (b) add newly identified obligations; (c) update status of completed events; (d) route material revisions (new compliance obligations or changes to due dates) through the standard document review cycle per RPL-DCC-PRO-003 Section 2; and (e) obtain re-approval from P03 for any version increment. An expedited update shall be triggered upon receipt of a new IURC order, rule amendment, or Notice of Proposed Rulemaking affecting RPL.

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:4.5 -->
**4.5 Status Codes.** Calendar events carry one of three status codes: **scheduled** (event has not yet occurred and is on track); **completed** (event action has been taken and documented); **n_a** (event has been determined to be not applicable in 2025, with written justification retained by owner). At document approval date (2025-03-14), Q1 events completed before that date are marked completed; all others are scheduled.

<!-- RPL-REG-CAL-2025:4.6 -->
**4.6 Escalation.** If an owner anticipates that a deadline will be missed, they must notify P08 (Manager Regulatory Affairs) at least 10 RPL business days before the due date. P08 will determine whether to escalate to P07 (VP Regulatory & Government Affairs), seek an extension from the IURC, or invoke RPL's REG-ONCALL paging group for after-hours coordination.
