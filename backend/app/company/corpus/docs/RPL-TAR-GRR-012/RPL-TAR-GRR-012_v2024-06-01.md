## Sheet 4 — Service Area
**Original Sheet 4 | Cause No. 98876 | Effective: 2019-07-01**

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:S04.1 -->
Rockridge Power & Light Company is authorized by the Indiana Utility Regulatory Commission to provide electric distribution service within its certificated service territory in the State of Indiana. The Company's service territory encompasses portions of the following fourteen (14) counties in west-central Indiana: Tippecanoe, Clinton, Carroll, White, Benton, Warren, Fountain, Montgomery, Boone, Hendricks, Putnam, Parke, Vermillion, and Vigo.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:S04.2 -->
Service is provided from five (5) service centers: Lafayette (LAF), Crawfordsville (CRW), Terre Haute (THT), Frankfort (FRK), and Danville (DAN). The exact boundaries of the certificated service territory are on file with the IURC and available for inspection at the Company's principal office at 400 Wabash Commons Drive, Lafayette, IN 47901.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:S04.3 -->
This tariff and all Rate Schedules herein apply only to service rendered within the certificated service territory of RPL pursuant to IC 8-1-2 and the applicable certificate of territorial authority on file with the Commission. Pursuant to 170 IAC 1-2-1, separate schedules are filed for each class of service.

## Sheets 5–11 — Rule 1: Definitions
**Original Sheets 5–9 | Cause No. 98876 | Effective: 2019-07-01**
**1st Revised Sheets 10–11 | Cause No. 98951 | Effective: 2021-10-01**

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D01 -->
**Section 1.1 — General.** The following definitions apply throughout this tariff. Where a term is defined in IC 8-1-2-1 or the NARUC Uniform System of Accounts (incorporated by reference at 170 IAC 4-2-2), that definition governs except as modified herein. Pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-1, the Commission's definitions for "customer," "disconnection," and "late payment charge" apply throughout this tariff.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D02 -->
**1.2 — Applicant.** Any person, firm, corporation, municipality, or other government agency that requests electric service from the Company at a new service location or at a location not previously served.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D03 -->
**1.3 — Customer.** Any person, firm, corporation, municipality, or other government agency that has agreed, orally or otherwise, to pay for electric service received from RPL. For purposes of Rules 10, 13.1(d), 13.2, and 13.3, the term shall be limited to persons who have agreed to pay for service exclusively for residential purposes, as defined in 170 IAC 4-1-1(c).

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D04 -->
**1.4 — Commission.** The Indiana Utility Regulatory Commission (IURC), PNC Center, 101 W. Washington Street, Suite 1500E, Indianapolis, IN 46204. Consumer Affairs Division: toll-free 1-800-851-4268; direct 317-232-2712; fax 317-233-2410; hours: 8:15 a.m.–4:45 p.m. ET, Monday–Friday; online: iurc.portal.in.gov; State Form 50488.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D05 -->
**1.5 — OUCC.** The Indiana Office of Utility Consumer Counselor, toll-free 1-888-441-2494.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D06 -->
**1.6 — Company / RPL.** Rockridge Power & Light Company, an Indiana investor-owned electric distribution utility subject to Commission jurisdiction under IC 8-1-2, IURC Utility ID 99012.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D07 -->
**1.7 — Disconnection.** The termination or discontinuance of electric service, as defined in 170 IAC 4-1-1(d).

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D08 -->
**1.8 — Delinquent Bill.** A bill for which payment has not been received within seventeen (17) days after the initial bill is postmarked, pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-13(c).

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D09 -->
**1.9 — Late Payment Charge.** A one-time penalty assessed upon all current bills when they become delinquent, as defined in 170 IAC 4-1-1(e). The charge shall not exceed ten percent (10%) of the first three dollars ($3) and three percent (3%) of the excess of three dollars ($3), pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-13(c).

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D10 -->
**1.10 — Service Entrance.** The point where the Company's distribution facilities connect to the customer's premises wiring, as determined by the Company in accordance with applicable construction standards and the National Electrical Safety Code (NESC).

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D11 -->
**1.11 — Point of Delivery.** The specific location designated by the Company for the metering and delivery of electric energy to the customer, which is the boundary of Company and customer ownership and maintenance responsibility.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D12 -->
**1.12 — Meter.** Any instrument or device owned and installed by the Company to measure the quantity of electric energy or demand consumed by a customer. This includes solid-state AMI (Advanced Metering Infrastructure) meters, solid-state AMR meters, and electromechanical meters.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D13 -->
**1.13 — AMI.** Advanced Metering Infrastructure; a system enabling two-way communication between the Company and customer meters, providing remote meter reading, remote connect/disconnect capability, and enhanced usage data. Company's AMI system operates under the CIS/AMI Head-End System (AMI_HES).

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D14 -->
**1.14 — Sustained Service Interruption.** A service interruption that is greater than or equal to five (5) minutes, as defined in 170 IAC 4-1-23(a)(10).

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D15 -->
**1.15 — Investor-Owned Utility (IOU).** A utility financed by the sale of securities and whose business operations are overseen by a board representing shareholders, as defined in 170 IAC 4-1-23(a)(6). RPL is an investor-owned utility.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D16 -->
**1.16 — Deposit.** A cash security payment required by the Company as a condition of providing or continuing service, pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-15.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D17 -->
**1.17 — Tariff.** This document, IURC Tariff No. 12, comprising the General Rules and Regulations and all Rate Schedules filed pursuant to 170 IAC 1-2-2, which is not complete unless it includes all rules, regulations, and formulas that in any wise affect the rates, tolls, or charges.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D18 -->
**1.18 — Standard Service Voltages.** The standard voltages at which RPL provides distribution service, as set forth in Rule 6 (Table V) of this tariff, filed with the Commission pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-20(A).

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D19 -->
**1.19 — Working Day.** Any day that is not a Saturday, Sunday, or a Company-observed holiday. Company holidays are listed in Section 1.21 herein.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D20 -->
**1.20 — Nonrecurring Charge.** A charge to a customer for costs incurred by the Company outside the context of month-to-month service, including but not limited to connection fees, reconnection fees, returned payment fees, and deposit-related fees, as defined in 170 IAC 1-6-2(11). All nonrecurring charges are set forth in Table F on Sheets 45–46.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D21 -->
**1.21 — Company Holidays 2025.** January 1, January 20, May 26, July 4, September 1, November 27, November 28, December 24, December 25. Holidays for subsequent years are posted at www.rockridge-pl.example and available upon request.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D22 -->
**1.22 — Financial Hardship.** A condition recognized as "cause" for inability to pay the full amount due, as provided in 170 IAC 4-1-16(c)(2). Financial hardship constitutes cause for a deferred payment arrangement.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D23 -->
**1.23 — Consumer Affairs.** The Consumer Affairs Division of the Indiana Utility Regulatory Commission, which handles customer complaints pursuant to 170 IAC 16-1-1 through 170 IAC 16-1-7.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D24 -->
**1.24 — Dispute.** Any disagreement between a customer and the Company regarding the amount of a utility bill, the applicability of a rate, the quality of service, or any other matter related to the provision of electric service, subject to resolution under 170 IAC 16-1-3 through 170 IAC 16-1-7.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D25 -->
**1.25 — LIHEAP.** The Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program administered under IC 4-4-33. Customers who receive or have applied for LIHEAP benefits are entitled to the winter disconnection protections set forth in Rule 13.6 and 170 IAC 4-1-16.6.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D26 -->
**1.26 — Budget Billing / Alternative Billing Method.** A billing arrangement under which the Company estimates the customer's annual electric service cost and bills the customer in equal monthly installments over a contract period, with a reconciliation at the end of the period. This method is offered pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-13(f) and the applicable Rate Schedule approved by the Commission.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D27 -->
**1.27 — Interconnection / Distributed Generation.** The physical and electrical connection of a customer-owned generating facility to RPL's distribution system. All distributed generation and net metering arrangements are governed by the applicable Rate Schedule (RS-30 through RS-32) and the interconnection agreement required under applicable Commission rules, IC 8-1-40, and FERC Order No. 2222.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D28 -->
**1.28 — Critical Facilities.** Premises that provide essential public health and safety services, including hospitals, fire stations, police stations, emergency communications centers, water and wastewater treatment facilities, and similar facilities identified by the customer. The Company maintains procedures for prioritizing restoration of critical facilities during sustained service interruptions pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-23(d).

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D29 -->
**1.29 — MISO.** The Midcontinent Independent System Operator, the regional transmission organization within whose footprint RPL purchases wholesale electric power pursuant to a FERC-jurisdictional full-requirements supply agreement. RPL's retail distribution rates are subject to IURC jurisdiction under IC 8-1-2, while MISO's transmission rates are subject to FERC jurisdiction.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R01.D30 -->
**1.30 — Field Collection Visit.** A visit by a Company representative to a customer's premises for the purpose of collecting payment on an overdue account, subject to the charge set forth in Table F (F-6: $20.00). The Company shall notify customers in the customer pamphlet of the conditions under which a field collection visit may be initiated, pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-18(A).

## Sheets 13–14 — Rule 3: Records
**Original Sheets 13–14 | Cause No. 98876 | Effective: 2019-07-01**

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R03.1 -->
**Section 3.1 — Records Retention.** All records required by Commission rules shall be preserved for at least three (3) years, except as otherwise provided by IC 8-1-2-40 or other applicable statutes, pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-3. Such records shall be kept within the State of Indiana at the Company's principal place of business at 400 Wabash Commons Drive, Lafayette, IN 47901, or at such other locations as the Company shall designate after notifying the Commission. All records shall be open for examination by the Commission or its authorized representatives.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R03.2 -->
**Section 3.2 — Meter Purchase and Test Records.** The Company shall maintain records of all meter tests as required by 170 IAC 4-1-4. For each meter test performed, a record shall be preserved identifying: (a) the meter; (b) the reason for the test; (c) the meter reading before the test; and (d) the results of the test, with all data necessary to permit calculation of average accuracy for billing adjustments. Permanent records shall be systematically maintained for each meter owned or used by the Company, showing the year of purchase, identification, and record of the last test.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R03.3 -->
**Section 3.3 — Meter Testing Equipment Records.** Records of certification and calibration of reference standards and portable testing equipment shall be kept on file at the Company's Meter Shop and Standards Laboratory, Lafayette HQ campus, pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-7(F). Reference standards shall be tested at least once every two (2) years by a recognized standardizing laboratory.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R03.4 -->
**Section 3.4 — Reliability Records.** The Company shall maintain historical CAIDI, SAIDI, SAIFI, and supporting data for a minimum of seven (7) years, pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-23(f). Annual reliability indices reports shall be filed with the Commission's electricity division on or before March 1 of each year, as required by 170 IAC 4-1-23(e).

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R03.5 -->
**Section 3.5 — Dispute Records.** The Company shall retain records of customer disputes and resolutions for a period of six (6) months from the date of final resolution, as required by 170 IAC 16-1-4(b). Such records shall include at minimum: the customer's name, service address, contact telephone number, account number, and the general nature of the dispute.

## Sheets 17–18 — Rule 5: Temporary Service
**Original Sheets 17–18 | Cause No. 98876 | Effective: 2019-07-01**

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R05.1 -->
**Section 5.1 — Definition.** Temporary service is electric service furnished to a customer for a definite, limited period, not to exceed twelve (12) months, for construction, seasonal, or other short-term needs.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R05.2 -->
**Section 5.2 — Charges for Temporary Service.** Temporary service is furnished only where facilities are available and at the Company's discretion. The customer shall bear the cost of temporary service installations and removals, including all material and labor costs for facilities that will not remain permanently useful to the Company's system.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R05.3 -->
**Section 5.3 — Advance Payment.** For temporary service, the Company may require advance payment of the estimated cost of service and installation before construction commences. Such advance payment is not a deposit subject to the interest provisions of Rule 10 but represents a contribution in aid of construction.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R05.4 -->
**Section 5.4 — Applicable Rate Schedule.** Temporary service shall be billed at the applicable Rate Schedule for the character of load served, plus any applicable nonrecurring charges set forth in Table F.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R05.5 -->
**Section 5.5 — Construction Service.** Temporary construction service is governed by Rate Schedule RS-47 (Construction Service) and RS-48 (Temporary Construction — Special). The Company shall provide safe and adequate temporary facilities to the extent permitted by available system capacity. When permanent service is established for the same premises, any temporary service contributions in aid of construction shall be credited against permanent service installation costs to the extent specified in the applicable Rate Schedule or line extension agreement.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R05.6 -->
**Section 5.6 — Removal of Temporary Facilities.** Upon expiration or termination of temporary service, the customer shall cooperate with the Company in removing temporary facilities. If the customer fails to coordinate removal, the Company may remove such facilities at the customer's expense after reasonable notice. Meters and other Company-owned equipment removed from temporary service locations are the property of the Company at all times.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R06.5 -->

Customers desiring primary voltage service (7,200/12,470V or 19,920/34,500V) shall provide and maintain suitable transformation equipment.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R06.6 -->
**Section 6.6 — System Monitoring Instruments.** The Company shall install and maintain in accurate working order such instruments as may be necessary to obtain daily records of frequency, voltage, kilowatt load, and kilowatt hours output of its purchasing points and to obtain complete information regarding monthly wholesale power purchases, pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-22 and IC 8-1-2-34.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R06.7 -->
**Section 6.7 — Pole Identification.** Each pole, post, or structure used for supporting electrical conductors shall be marked with the Company's abbreviation or corporate symbol and a numerical identifier per 170 IAC 4-1-25(A). Markings shall be of sufficient size to be easily read from the ground, as required by 170 IAC 4-1-25(B). In cases of jointly owned structures, the distinguishing mark of each owner shall appear, pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-25(C).

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R06.8 -->
**Section 6.8 — Line Construction.** The Company's distribution facilities shall be designed, constructed, and maintained in accordance with applicable state and national standards including the National Electrical Safety Code (NESC), applicable Commission rules including 170 IAC 4-1-26 and 170 IAC 4-1-27 through 170 IAC 4-1-30, and the orders and directives of the Commission. The Commission may grant variances from construction standards upon application by the utility.

## Sheets 27–29 — Rule 9: Metering
**2nd Revised Sheets 27–29 | Cause No. 99012 | Effective: 2024-06-01**

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R09.1 -->
**Section 9.1 — Company-Owned Meters.** All meters used to measure and record electric service for billing purposes are owned by the Company. Pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-5(A), all meters shall be located outdoors where practicable; where outdoor installation is impractical, meters shall be located indoors as near as possible to the service entrance in a clean, dry, safe place.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R09.2 -->
**Section 9.2 — Meter Installation Standards.** Pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-5(C), meters shall be easily accessible for reading, testing, and making necessary adjustments and repairs. When multiple meters are placed on the same meter board, the distance between centers shall be not less than 7-1/2 inches. Meters shall not be less than four (4) feet nor more than six (6) feet above the final standing surface, measured from the center of the meter cover, unless otherwise authorized by the Company.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R09.3 -->
**Section 9.3 — New Meter Inspection and Testing.** Pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-6(a), each new watthour meter, except self-contained single-phase and network meters, shall be inspected and tested prior to installation to verify accuracy, correct constants, and that the meter does not register with all load wires disconnected. All new meters may be tested by a meter manufacturer if certified tests are supplied. Pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-6(c), all watthour and demand meters, except self-contained meters, shall be tested prior to or within sixty (60) days of installation and adjusted to the condition of zero error within the tolerance limits specified in Section 9.4.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R09.4 -->
**Section 9.4 — Meter Accuracy Standards.** Pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-9(b), no meter shall be placed in service or allowed to remain in service unless it meets the following accuracy requirements:

(a) **Watthour meters:** Average error not over two percent (2%), plus or minus; error at full load not over one percent (1%), plus or minus; error at light load not over three percent (3%), plus or minus. [170 IAC 4-1-9(b)(1)]

(b) **Integrating demand meters:** Electric element errors shall not exceed the limits specified for watthour meters; timing element cumulative error shall not exceed two percent (2%) for the entire billing period; if time of day is a rate factor, the timing element shall not indicate a difference of more than ten (10) minutes from correct time. [170 IAC 4-1-9(b)(3)]

(c) **No-load test:** No watthour meter that registers at no load when the applied voltage is less than 110% of standard service voltage shall be placed in or allowed to remain in service. [170 IAC 4-1-9(a)]

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R09.5 -->
**Section 9.5 — Average Accuracy Determination.** Pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-8, the average accuracy of a meter shall be the average of the percentage registration at light load (approximately 10% of rated test amperes) and at full load (approximately 100% of rated test amperes). Average accuracy = (FL + LL) ÷ 2. Light load accuracy shall be determined by taking the average of at least two (2) tests that agree within one-half of one percent (0.5%), unless tested by an automated device, in which case one (1) test will suffice.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R09.6 -->
**Section 9.6 — Periodic In-Service Testing.** The Company shall test meters in service pursuant to the schedules required by 170 IAC 4-1-10:

(a) **Method A (Periodic Testing):** Self-contained meters shall be tested on a schedule of not more than sixteen (16) years. [170 IAC 4-1-10(b)]

(b) **Method B (Quality Control):** As an alternative, the Company may adopt the quality control testing method described in 170 IAC 4-1-10(c), with upper and lower accuracy specification limits of 102% and 98% respectively. [170 IAC 4-1-10(c)(5)]

(c) **Electronic meters:** Shall be tested at least every sixteen (16) years. [170 IAC 4-1-10(d)(2)]

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R09.7 -->
**Section 9.7 — Customer-Requested Meter Tests.** Pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-11:

(a) The Company shall test the accuracy of any meter upon written request by the customer. A second test may be requested after twelve (12) months. The first and second customer-requested tests shall be at no charge to the customer. [170 IAC 4-1-11(a)]

(b) The customer may be required to bear the reasonable cost of subsequent tests if the meter was tested within the prior thirty-six (36) months at the customer's request, found to be in compliance, and is again found in compliance. The Company shall disclose the test cost to the customer prior to testing. The applicable charge is set forth in Table F (F-9: $40.00 residential / $95.00 polyphase). [170 IAC 4-1-11(b)-(c)]

(c) A written report of the test results shall be provided to the customer within ten (10) days after the test is complete, and a complete record shall be maintained in the Company's files. [170 IAC 4-1-11(d)]

(d) Any appeal regarding the results of the customer's meter test shall be filed with the Commission within five (5) days of the date of the written report. [170 IAC 4-1-11(e)]

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R09.8 -->
**Section 9.8 — Commission-Supervised Tests.** Upon application of any customer to the Commission, a test of the customer's watthour meter shall be made by RPL or its contractor under the supervision of a Commission employee, pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-12(a). No fee shall be payable by the customer for such a test, except as may be charged under Section 9.7(b). Upon receiving notice of such application, the Company shall not remove, interfere with, or discard the customer's meter until completion of the test without the customer's prior written consent, unless removal is necessary to perform the test, pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-12(c).

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R09.9 -->
**Section 9.9 — AMI Program; Opt-Out.** The Company's standard meter is an AMI solid-state meter. A residential customer who does not wish to have an AMI meter installed may opt out by requesting a standard (non-AMI) meter by contacting the Company at 1-800-555-0142. The applicable opt-out charges are set forth in Table F (F-11: one-time $50.00 and monthly $18.00). Upon customer request, the Company shall provide the customer with the number of the meter serving the individual customer's premises, pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-5(C).

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R09.10 -->
**Section 9.10 — Meter Testing Equipment.** The Company shall provide or have available standard meters, instruments, and other equipment as may be necessary to make tests required by Commission rules, pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-7(B). The Company's Meter Shop and Standards Laboratory is located at the Lafayette HQ campus. Reference standards shall be tested and adjusted, if necessary, at least once every two (2) years by a recognized standardizing laboratory, pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-7(C).

## Sheets 37–41 — Rule 13: Disconnection

(b) Upon order by any court, the Commission, or other duly authorized public authority; [170 IAC 4-1-16(b)(2)]

(c) If fraudulent or unauthorized use of electricity is detected and the Company has reasonable grounds to believe the affected customer is responsible; [170 IAC 4-1-16(b)(3)]

(a) The date of the proposed disconnection; [170 IAC 4-1-16(e)(1)]

[170 IAC 4-1-16(c)(2)]

One additional ten (10) day postponement is available upon provision of an additional medical statement.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R13.8 -->
**Section 13.8 — Pre-Disconnection Procedures.** Pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-16(f), immediately preceding the actual disconnection, the Company's designated employee shall: (1) make a reasonable attempt to identify themselves to any customer or responsible person on the premises; (2) announce the purpose of their presence; (3) make a record to be maintained for at least thirty (30) days; (4) have information sufficient to inform the customer of the reason for disconnection, including the amount of any delinquent bill; and (5) request any available verification that the outstanding bill has been satisfied or is currently in dispute. **Section 13.9 — Reconnection Requirements.** Pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-16(g), the Company may charge a reconnection fee as set forth in Table F. The Company shall reconnect service as soon as reasonably possible but at least within one (1) working day after the customer has satisfied all conditions for reconnection. If the Company disconnects service in violation of Commission Rule 170 IAC 4-1-16, service shall be immediately restored at no charge to the customer. Reconnection charges are set forth in Table F: F-1 (Remote reconnect: $10.00), F-2 (Field reconnect regular hours: $45.00), F-3 (Field reconnect after-hours weekday: $70.00), F-4 (Field reconnect Sat/Sun/holiday: $120.00), F-5 (Reconnect at pole/transformer/vault: $165.00).

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R13.10 -->
**Section 13.10 — Customer Complaint Process.** Any customer who disagrees with the Company's proposed resolution of a billing or service dispute may appeal to the IURC Consumer Affairs Division, pursuant to 170 IAC 16-1-4(c)(5), within seven (7) days of receiving the Company's proposed resolution. The Company shall inform customers of their right to appeal and shall provide Consumer Affairs contact information. Complaints to Consumer Affairs may be filed by telephone (1-800-851-4268), in writing, or by completing State Form 50488 available at iurc.portal.in.gov. Consumer Affairs shall provide a decision within thirty (30) days of the complaint submission date, pursuant to 170 IAC 16-1-5(c)(5). Either party may request Commission review of the Consumer Affairs decision within twenty (20) days, pursuant to 170 IAC 16-1-6(a).

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R13.11 -->
**Section 13.11 — Service Protection During Dispute.** If a customer has paid and continues to pay all undisputed charges, the Company shall not disconnect service related to disputed charges while the dispute is under review by Consumer Affairs or the Commission, or sooner than ten (10) days after a decision by Consumer Affairs or the Commission, pursuant to 170 IAC 16-1-7(a). If the parties cannot agree on the undisputed amount, to avoid disconnection the customer should pay an amount equal to one-twelfth (1/12) of the estimated annual billing, pursuant to 170 IAC 16-1-7(b).

## Sheets 42–43 — Rule 14: Service Interruptions
**Original Sheets 42–43 | Cause No. 98876 | Effective: 2019-07-01**

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R14.1 -->
**Section 14.1 — Reporting of Major Interruptions.** Pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-23(b)(1)(A), RPL shall report any unplanned interruption in service lasting two (2) hours or more and affecting two percent (2%) or five thousand (5,000) customers, whichever is fewer. An initial report shall be made to the Commission by the next regularly scheduled reporting interval; updates shall be made at each subsequent interval until service is restored below the threshold.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R14.2 -->
**Section 14.2 — Reporting Schedule.** Pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-23(b)(2), reporting intervals are as follows: (a) Business days: 6:00 a.m., 9:00 a.m., 11:00 a.m., 2:00 p.m., 4:00 p.m., and 9:00 p.m. EST (Indianapolis time); (b) Nonbusiness days: 6:00 a.m., 2:00 p.m., and 9:00 p.m. EST. Reports shall be submitted to the Commission and OUCC via Commission-prescribed format, preferably by electronic mail, pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-23(b)(3).

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R14.3 -->
**Section 14.3 — Emergency Procedures.** The Company shall maintain written procedures for designated employees to follow in emergencies, containing at minimum: (1) notification procedures for emergency response personnel; (2) general location of equipment, tools, and materials; and (3) procedures for notifying fire, police, medical, and other public officials, pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-23(d).

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R14.4 -->
**Section 14.4 — Advance Notice of Planned Interruptions.** For scheduled maintenance or facilities upgrades, the Company shall make reasonable attempts to notify customers whose service is expected to be interrupted for more than one (1) hour, consistent with safety and security considerations, pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-23(c).

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R14.5 -->
**Section 14.5 — Reliability Indices Annual Report.** Pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-23(e), RPL shall file a reliability indices report with the Commission's electricity division on or before March 1 of each year, including SAIDI, CAIDI, and SAIFI indices with and without major events, for the system and for each district. The Company shall maintain historical data for at least seven (7) years pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-23(f).

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R14.7 -->
**Section 14.7 — Public Health and Safety Priority.** During service restoration activities following any sustained service interruption, the Company shall first attempt to restore service to facilities that affect public health and safety, pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-23(d). Public health and safety facilities include, without limitation, hospitals, emergency communications centers, water treatment plants, and critical government facilities. The Company's Distribution Control Center (located in Lafayette, staffed 24/7) coordinates all restoration activities and maintains escalation procedures including DCC-ESCALATION on-call protocols.

<!-- RPL-TAR-GRR-012:R14.6 -->
**Section 14.6 — Accident Reports.** Pursuant to 170 IAC 4-1-24 and IC 8-1-2-114, whenever an accident attended with loss of human life occurs on or in connection with Company premises or operations, the Company shall give immediate notice to the Commission. If the accident occurs during regular business hours, the Commission shall be notified by telephone with pertinent details including the name of the deceased. If the accident occurs outside regular business hours, the Commission shall be notified as early as practicable on the first business day following the accident. Written reports shall follow as soon as all pertinent information is accumulated.
